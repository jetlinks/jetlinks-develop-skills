# Systematic Solving

Preserve the user's original outcome, accepted constraints and existing authorization. Solve the demonstrated class of problems at its owning boundary; do not invent a broader abstraction or a new approval step merely because the work spans files.

## Admission

This is the canonical admission rule for routers and domain skills. Use this skill when evidence leaves materially different causal explanations, a shared / high-impact boundary lacks a decided contract, or diagnostic patches keep failing, moving the symptom or adding unsupported exceptions. Complexity labels, architecture-layer counts and technology keywords are only hints.

An already-decided cross-layer change can proceed directly with proportionate validation. Implementing an explicitly requested retry policy, writing a test double, or editing several files in one coherent solution is normal implementation. These actions become stagnation signals only in an active diagnosis when they mask an unchanged unexplained failure, replace the disputed boundary with a mock, or repeat a check with no possible new information. Mechanical assembly fixes do not require a new root-cause theory.

## Work from evidence

1. State the unresolved decision, known facts, affected invariant and acceptance signal. Separate factual uncertainty from a material product choice. Only open a `SemanticFork` when multiple plausible contracts change ownership, persistence, security, public behavior or another durable boundary; do not manufacture options for a determined local implementation.
2. Build the smallest producer–boundary–consumer map needed to distinguish the candidates. Use [code-navigation](../code-navigation/SKILL.md) only when ownership or the path is not already known. Give a diagnostic observation its hypothesis, bounded scope, expected discriminator and stop condition.
3. Classify observations: `DISCRIMINATING` supports the linked decision; `INVALID` failed to reach the boundary; `INCONCLUSIVE` cannot distinguish candidates; `SCOPE_INVALID` cannot decide the user's contract. The latter three cannot authorize a solution-changing mutation. They support a bounded observation repair, a better check, reframing or a real blocker.
4. Stop investigation when evidence supports the decision or identifies the one user choice that remains. Ask only for that missing material choice; reuse decisions already supplied in the conversation. While a material fork is `OPEN`, do not implement, publish authoritative design or review a candidate as if its contract were accepted.
5. Partition failures into production defect, stale consumer/oracle, invalid fixture, mechanical assembly or unresolved cause. Fix each at its owner. After an informative failure, compare it with the prediction before another patch; retain a supported hypothesis when the failure identifies an incomplete implementation, and revise it when evidence contradicts or narrows it. Do not accumulate symptom-specific fallbacks under an unexplained cause.
6. Make the smallest complete change for the demonstrated invariant and variation axis. Guard untrusted input, authoritative permissions, state/persistence invariants or dangerous operations once at the owning boundary; do not copy framework checks across layers or add guards to satisfy a test checklist.
7. Validate at coherent stage boundaries. Cover the bug trigger, a sibling when shared behavior changed, a counterexample when boundary risk changed, and affected regressions. Reuse still-valid evidence; do not repeatedly build after individual edits.

Preserve the current goal when the user asks a question or repeats a reminder; answer and continue. Revisit only the obligations affected by a new constraint or decision. Do not create a formal observation or user-message ledger for ordinary implementation or every conversational turn.

## Details only when needed

- For competing hypotheses, semantic-fork resolution or a bounded diagnostic retry, read the relevant section of [systematic-solving-rules.md](references/systematic-solving-rules.md). Its admission section owns the detailed trigger boundary; domain skills should reference it instead of copying triggers.
- For work that actually spans recovery or handoff, use an available continuity capability such as [task-continuity](../task-continuity/SKILL.md). For independent bounded work, use [agent-orchestration](../agent-orchestration/SKILL.md). Neither is a prerequisite for a local diagnosis.
- For maintenance, use [evaluation-cases.md](references/evaluation-cases.md) and [evaluate_systematic_trace.py](scripts/evaluate_systematic_trace.py); read [research-basis.md](references/research-basis.md) when changing the policy. Investigation counts are diagnostic defaults, not substitutes for correctness.

Report the supported cause or decision, resulting behavior, material evidence and unresolved risk. Include hypotheses or control-state details only when they explain the decision or were requested.
